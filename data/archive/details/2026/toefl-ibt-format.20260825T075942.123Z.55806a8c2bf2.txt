ETS TOEFL iBT Test Content and Structure, checked 2026-08-25
Effective 2026-01-21. Reading 50 items / 30 min, Listening 47 / 29 min,
Writing 12 / 23 min, Speaking 11 / 8 min. Base total 120 items / 90 min.
Reading and Listening are adaptive; time and items may vary. Directions excluded.
Scores: four section and overall scores on 1-6 scale in half-band increments;
comparable overall 0-120 score is also provided for a two-year transition.

JLPT N1-N5 official detail evidence, checked 2026-08-25

Current forced test times:
N1 language knowledge/reading 110 min + listening 55 min.
N2 language knowledge/reading 105 min + listening 50 min.
N3 vocabulary 30 min + grammar/reading 70 min + listening 40 min.
N4 vocabulary 25 min + grammar/reading 55 min + listening 35 min.
N5 vocabulary 20 min + grammar/reading 40 min + listening 30 min.
Listening time can vary slightly with recorded material.

All levels are scored 0-180. Overall pass marks are N1 100, N2 90,
N3 95, N4 90, N5 80. N1-N3 require at least 19/60 in each of
language knowledge, reading and listening. N4-N5 require at least 38/120
in language knowledge/reading and 19/60 in listening. Missing any test
section is a fail. Certificates do not expire, though accepting institutions
may impose their own recency requirement.

The official site discloses test-item types, but not a fixed total question
count; answer correctness counts are not disclosed. No question count was
inferred from prep services. Current Korean regular fees for the official
2026 administration are KRW 75,000 for N1-N3 and KRW 60,000 for N4-N5.
Late application adds 10%.

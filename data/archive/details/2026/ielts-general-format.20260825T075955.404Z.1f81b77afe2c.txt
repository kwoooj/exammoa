IELTS General Training official format, checked 2026-08-25
Listening: 4 parts, 40 questions, approximately 30 minutes.
General Training Reading: 3 sections, 40 questions, 60 minutes.
General Training Writing: 2 tasks, 60 minutes; Task 2 has double weighting.
Speaking: 3 parts, 11-14 minutes. Scores use 0-9 bands.
British Council Korea computer test fee: KRW 347,000.
